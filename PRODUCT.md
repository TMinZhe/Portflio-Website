# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React / Next.js, deployed on Vercel at a default `vercel.app` URL. No custom domain yet.

## Users

Internship recruiters and hiring managers screening NUS candidates for software engineering, data/analytics/BI, and AI/ML internships. They are often skimming many candidates quickly. Their job is to decide, in a short visit, whether to shortlist the candidate and reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for an NUS student seeking internships. It presents projects and experience across software engineering, data/analytics/BI, and AI/ML. Success means a recruiter leaves able to say what the candidate is good at, has seen proof, and contacts them or saves the resume.

## Positioning

The business + tech bridge: the candidate turns data and code into decisions that business stakeholders actually use. The work should be framed by the problem, the stakeholder, and the outcome, not only the tech stack. Other candidates can list the same tools; this claim depends on showing that the work changed a decision or a workflow.

## Operating Context

- Recruiters arrive from a resume link, LinkedIn, or an application form, on desktop or mobile, and often spend under a minute.
- They usually want a resume download, contact details, and a quick read of the strongest projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site covers three areas: software engineering, data/analytics/BI (including Power BI), and AI/ML.
- Open: the owner's display name, bio, and contact links are not yet confirmed.
- Open: which projects appear, and in what order.

## Evidence on Hand

None supplied yet. The owner asked for placeholders for now.

- Every project, metric, employer, resume, screenshot, headshot, and link is a **placeholder** until the owner supplies the real one.
- Placeholders must be obviously marked as placeholders in the content and code (e.g. `[Project title]`, `TODO`). They must never read as real claims.
- Never invent employers, outcomes, metrics, testimonials, or awards.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every claim about skill points to a project, a role, or an artifact.
2. **Outcome first.** Lead each project with the business problem and result, then the tech.
3. **Scannable in under a minute.** The strongest evidence, the resume, and contact details are reachable without hunting.
4. **Honest placeholders.** Missing content is marked as missing, never filled with plausible fiction.
